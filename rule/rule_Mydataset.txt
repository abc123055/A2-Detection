**Rules for Anomaly Human Activities:**
1. Fighting, hitting, or pushing another person
2. Aggressive body movements or physical altercation
3. Running or chasing another person
4. Falling or being knocked to the ground
5. Grabbing or pulling another person forcefully

**Rules for Anomaly Environmental Objects:**
1. Objects being thrown or used as weapons
2. Broken or damaged items on the ground
3. Scattered personal belongings

**Rules for Normal Human Activities:**
1. Walking alone or with another person
2. Standing still or waiting
3. Sitting on a bench or chair
4. Talking to another person calmly
5. Carrying bags or personal items

**Rules for Normal Environmental Objects:**
1. Cars parked or driving on the street
2. Trees, buildings, and street infrastructure
3. Pedestrians walking on sidewalks
